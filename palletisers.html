Robot palletiser

One robot, several lines and formats

A pick and place robot in a modular cell. Stacking patterns change quickly and a new product is a matter of programming. We design and build the gripper head ourselves and test every cell in our factory, with your own products.

Products
Cases, trays and crates. Also empty bottles and kegs
Robots
ABB, Fanuc, Kuka or Yaskawa
Grippers
Clamp, vacuum or magnet, also combined
Options
Multi-lane infeed, pallet dispensers, one robot for several lines

Qimarox palletiser

High output on a small footprint

Van Uitert is a Qimarox partner. We place the palletiser in your line and build everything around it, so you get one working cell with one control.

Capacity
Up to 4,380 products per hour with dual infeed
Models
Lowrunner and Highrunner
Patterns
Open and chimney patterns, motor-driven layer guides
Our scope
Infeed conveyors, pallet and layer sheet magazines, pallet conveyors, guarding and PLC control. Labellers, wrappers and strappers integrated
