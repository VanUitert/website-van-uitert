About us

A family company that builds lines.

Since 1962 in Dongen, the Netherlands. We design, build, test and install everything with our own people.

Everything in-house

92 people under one roof

Our own design department, software, hardware and robot engineers, a workshop and our own fitters. That is why we can react fast and why one team stays on your project from the first layout to start-up.

  • Design, production and assembly in Dongen
  • Lines tested in our workshop before they ship
  • Installed and started up by our own fitters
Our history

From steel construction to line integrator

  • 1962
    The startVan Uitert starts as a construction company. One of the first products: greenhouses for export to the United States.
  • Late 1960s
    Conveyors and special machinesThe company chooses conveying systems and special machines. That choice is still the base of what we do.
  • Today
    Line integratorComplete filling and packaging lines for customers in 40 countries, built by 92 people in Dongen.
