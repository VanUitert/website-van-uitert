Build a new line

From empty hall to running line

We turn separate machines into one line that works as a whole. You choose the filler or packer you want. We do the engineering, the conveying, the handling and the line control around it.

  • One contact for the complete line
  • Machines of any brand, connected to one line control
  • Built and tested in Dongen before it comes to your plant

Improve a line

More out of the line you have

Your line has to run faster, handle a new product or stop less often. We look at the whole line first and then change what gives the most result. Existing machines stay where they can.

  • Higher capacity, new formats or fewer stops
  • Start with a line audit: where does the line lose time?
  • Work planned around your production stops

Move a line

Same line, new place

We take your line apart, move it to the new hall or the new plant and start it up again. A move is also the moment to change the layout or replace what is worn.

  • Dismantling, transport, installation and start-up
  • New layout for the new location
  • Upgrade during the move if you want
