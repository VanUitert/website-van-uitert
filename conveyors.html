Primary packaging

Bottles, cans and jars

Conveyors for glass, cans and plastic that distribute, combine and buffer the product flow. Stainless steel frames, low-friction chains and belts, running dry where the line allows it.

Capacity
Up to 120,000 packages per hour
Pressureless inliner
From mass flow to single file, up to 65,000 products per hour
Outliner
Up to 120,000 products per hour
Inline divider
From one lane to several, servo driven, up to 60 metres per minute
Buffers
Pressureless and first in, first out. Mass buffer up to 36 metres long
Also
Can twisters, elevators and lowerators
Secondary packaging

Cases, trays, crates and multipacks

Belt, chain and roller conveyors for full and empty packs. Accumulating where packs have to wait, with dividers, turners and buffers where the line needs them.

Packs
Cases, trays, crates and multipacks
Types
Belt conveyors, crate chain conveyors and driven, gravity or accumulating roller conveyors
Modules
Dividers, turners, crate buffers, elevators and lowerators
Build
Stainless steel frame, SEW drives

Pallets

From palletiser to warehouse

Roller conveyors carry pallets lengthwise, chain conveyors carry them crosswise. Around them we build what the route needs: turning stations, transfer cars, lifts and pallet checks.

Load
Pallets up to 1,500 kg
Capacity
Up to 200 pallets per hour
Types
Roller conveyors and chain conveyors
Modules
Turning station, transfer car, pallet lift, pallet check
Stacker and destacker
Up to 15 pallets high, 120 pallets per hour
