What we do · Build a new line

A new line, one party responsible.

You choose the filler or packer you want. We design the line around it, build our own machines, integrate the others and start it up.

Line integration

From empty hall to running line

We turn separate machines into one line that works as a whole. You get one contract, one planning and one contact for the complete line.

  • One contact for the complete line
  • Machines of any brand, connected to one line control
  • Built and tested in Dongen before it comes to your plant

What we take care of

You decide how much of the line you place with us. Most customers choose the complete line.

Line designLayout, capacities and buffering, made for your products and your hall.
Our own machinesDepalletisers, rinsers, dryers, conveyors, robot packers and palletisers from Dongen.
Machines of other brandsFillers, labellers, packers and wrappers. We connect them to the line and to the control.
