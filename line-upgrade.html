More out of the line you have.

Your line has to run faster, handle a new product or stop less often. We look at the whole line first and then change what gives the most result.

Existing machines stay where they can. We add, replace or rebuild the parts that hold the line back, and we plan the work around your production stops.

Where to start

A line audit

Not sure where your line loses time? In a line audit we record the technical state, the capacity and the bottlenecks of your line in one report. You decide what you do with it.

Ask for a line audit
  • Technical stateWhat is worn, what is outdated and what can stay.
  • CapacityWhat each machine and each conveyor can handle.
  • BottlenecksWhere the line stops and what that costs in output.
