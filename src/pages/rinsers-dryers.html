Rinsers and air cleaners

Turned, cleaned and turned back

The container is clamped between two gripper chains and turned 180 degrees. Loose particles fall out and the inside is cleaned with water or with ionised air, which takes away the static charge that makes dust stick. A second wheel turns the container upright again, on its way to the filler.

Cleaning
Water, air or ionised air
Products
Glass bottles, PET bottles, jars and cans
Size
Diameter 35 to 200 mm, height 50 to 350 mm
Versions
S-rinser, horizontal or vertical. C-rinser, vertical
Format change
Height and width adjustable, by hand or automatic
Capacity
Depends on the product. Delivered for lines up to 600 containers per minute

Product dryers

Dry without touching the product

A blower feeds stainless steel air knives that form a curtain of air at high speed and low pressure. The top is dried first, then the sides. The dryer uses ambient air and is a stand-alone module, so it fits in a new or an existing line.

Capacity
Up to 70,000 bottles or 90,000 cans per hour
Products
Cans, bottles, jars, plastic products, pouches, kegs and crates
Size
Products from 50 to 800 mm
Filling
Cold and hot filled
Build
Stainless steel, removable conveyor belt, large openings for cleaning, blower inside the housing
