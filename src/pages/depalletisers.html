Depalletisers.

The start of your line. Empty bottles, cans or jars come off the pallet layer by layer and go to the line in a controlled way. Which type fits depends on your speed, your products and your layout.

High-speed depalletiser

Fully automatic, servo driven

Pallets come in on a roller conveyor. The load is enclosed in a shaft and centred, so nothing can fall. Sheets go to the sheet magazine and empty pallets are stacked and carried off.

Capacity
Up to 6 layers per minute
Products
Cans, glass bottles and PET bottles, diameter 50 to 200 mm
Format change
Without change parts
Options
Empty pallet stacker, automatic adjustment to pallet size, handling of top frames and inverted trays

Compact depalletiser

Small footprint

The operator places the pallet in the lift shaft and closes the door. Each layer is pushed onto the buffer table and the interlayer sheet is taken off. Semi-automatic, and modular so you can extend it later.

Capacity
Up to 3 layers per minute
Products
Glass, cans and plastic: bottles, jars and cans
Pallet
Up to 1.5 metres high
Outfeed
Pressureless inliner or mass conveying
Low-level depalletiser

No platforms in your line

The pallet goes in at floor level with a pallet truck. A lift brings every layer to table height, where top frames and sheets are taken off by an arm. All handling is at working height.

Capacity
Up to 2 layers per minute
Products
Glass, PET and cans
Sheets and frames
Removed automatically, pneumatic or servo driven
Robot depalletiser

When products and formats change often

A robot unloads pallets with crates or with packaging such as glass, plastic and cans. We design and build the gripper head ourselves, for your product. Every robot cell is tested in our factory before delivery.

Grippers
Vacuum, mechanical, magnetic or push-off
Also handles
Interlayer sheets, top trays and empty pallets
