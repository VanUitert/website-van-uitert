Robot packers.

A pick and place robot puts your products in the crate, case or tray. Fast, careful and the same every time.

Robot packer

Formed first, then placed

The products are collated in the right pattern. The robot picks them up with a clamp or vacuum head and places them in the pack without damage. We build the head for your product, also when it is unstable or has an irregular shape. The same cell can unpack.

Capacity
Up to 10 strokes per minute
Packs
Crates, cases and trays
Products
Bottles, cans, bags and cases
Heads
Tulip grippers, bottle clamps or vacuum, with or without spreader
Options
Multi-lane infeed, several pack positions, crate stacker and destacker
