The question

A new line where the old one stood

The brewery needed a new line for returnable glass bottles in crates, with a nominal capacity of 40,000 bottles per hour. The existing line had to go first. The washer, filler and labeller came from other suppliers.

What we built

Five robots, one line

One robot takes the crates off the pallets. One takes the empty bottles out of the crates and one puts the full bottles back. Two robots stack the full crates on pallets.

  • All bottle, crate and pallet conveyors
  • Crate inspection and pallet storage
  • Integration with the filler and labeller
  • Built in 2007 and 2008, and still running
  • Still maintained and modernised by Van Uitert
