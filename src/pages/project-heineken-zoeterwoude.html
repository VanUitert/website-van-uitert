The question

Pallets from the lines to the trucks

The brewery in Zoeterwoude ships a large volume every day. Full pallets have to go from the packaging lines to the loading docks without waiting and without forklifts crossing each other.

What we built

One crossdock system

We designed, built and installed the pallet conveying of the crossdock, including the control.

  • Kept up to date by the same team
