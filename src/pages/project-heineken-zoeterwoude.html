The question

Pallets from the lines to the trucks

The brewery in Zoeterwoude ships a large volume every day. Full pallets have to go from the packaging lines to the loading docks without waiting and without forklifts crossing each other.

What we built
  • About 460 metres of driven pallet chain conveyors
  • Transfer cars with turning stations
  • Control for 99 motors, linked to the warehouse system of Heineken
  • Design, build, installation and start-up by Van Uitert
  • Started up in 2008, extended several times since
