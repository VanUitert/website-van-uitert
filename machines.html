Machines

What we build ourselves.

These machines come from our own workshop in Dongen. Every other machine in your line can be any brand: we integrate it.

01Depalletising02Rinsing and drying04Conveying05Packing06Palletising
Station 01

Depalletising

The start of your line. Empty bottles, cans or jars are taken off the pallet automatically and fed to the line in a controlled way.

  • DepalletiserEmpties pallets layer by layer, as the start of your production process.
  • Robot depalletiserFor lines where products and pack formats change often.
Station 02

Rinsing and drying

Clean containers before filling and dry products before labelling or packing.

  • Rinsers and air cleanersClean and rinse containers before filling, with water or air.
  • Product dryersDry products or packs after rinsing or filling, before the next step.
Station 04

Conveying

The link between every machine in your line. We build conveyors for three kinds of load.

  • Primary packagingBottles, jars, cans, cartons and kegs.
  • Secondary packagingCases, trays, crates and multipacks.
  • PalletsPallets, big bags, trolleys and IBC containers.
Station 05

Packing

A robot that places your products in the pack, fast and the same every time.

  • Robot packerPlaces products in crates, cases or trays.
Station 06

Palletising

The end of your line. Cases, crates and trays are stacked on pallets, ready for the warehouse. We build the complete cell: infeed, pallet magazine, layer sheets, pallet conveying, safety and control.

  • Robot palletiserFlexible stacking patterns, for a changing product mix.
  • Qimarox palletiserUp to 4,380 packs per hour, in a low-level or high-level version. Van Uitert is a Qimarox partner.
